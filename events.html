Bollywood Day

Category: Cultural

Date: 11/02/2026

Students dressed up as iconic Bollywood characters from movies like 3 Idiots, classic retro films, and modern blockbusters. Everyone showcased unique costumes, participated in a Bollywood ramp walk, and recreated iconic dialogue deliveries and group dance performances.

IT Project Expo

Category: Academic

Date: 22/09/2026

Students showcased innovative IT and Computer Application projects, including interactive web developments (HTML/CSS/JS), robotics automation, smart hardware models, and software utility applications built during the semester.

Annual Sports Days

Category: Sports

Date: 09/02/2026

An action-packed sports festival featuring energetic inter-class competitions such as tug of war, 100m sprint races, badminton, chess, and traditional games like sui-dhaaga tournaments.

Traditional Day Festival

Category: Cultural

Date: 14/02/2026

A celebration of cultural diversity where students wore vibrant ethnic attire, sarees, kurtas, and traditional regional outfits. The event featured folk dance performances, traditional music shows, and various cultural stalls.

Color Day

Category: Cultural

Date: 12/02/2026

Everyone attended the event wearing stylish black shirts and grey pants in party wear themes. All the students and faculty looked incredibly stylish and well-dressed, creating a classy theme and vibrant atmosphere across the campus.

Food Festival Day

Category: Cultural

Date: 10/02/2026

Students set up various delicious food stalls including Pani Puri, Cold Coffee, snacks, and refreshing drinks. It was a hugely successful food fair where everyone enjoyed tasting different dishes and hanging out with friends.

Women's Day Celebration

Category: Cultural

Date: 08/03/2026

A grand celebration honoring female students, faculty, and staff members, showcasing women empowerment programs, cultural speeches, and special performances across the campus.

Fresher's Party

Category: Cultural

Date: 13/08/2025

A grand welcome party organized by senior students for incoming first-year freshers. The event included talent showcases, music, dance performances, and Mr. & Ms. Fresher titles.

Farewell Party

Category: Cultural

Date: 01/04/2026

A memorable farewell gathering held to honor final year graduating students. Juniors organized emotional video tributes, fun awards, games, and speeches to give a grand send-off.

Teacher's Day Celebration

Category: Academic

Date: 05/09/2026

Students took over teaching roles for the day to express gratitude toward faculty members. Speeches, flower presentations, and entertaining cultural games were hosted for professors.

Independence Day Celebration

Category: Cultural

Date: 15/08/2026

Official flag hoisting ceremony followed by patriotic song performances, NCC parades, and inspiring addresses celebrating national pride and unity across the college premises.
